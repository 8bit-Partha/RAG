# Acceptable Use Policy

## Section 1: Scope
This policy applies to all use of company-owned or company-managed devices,
networks, and accounts by employees, contractors, and interns.

## Section 2: Permitted Use
Company systems are provided primarily for business purposes. Limited
personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with job
performance, consume excessive resources, or violate any other company
policy.

## Section 3: Prohibited Activities
The following are strictly prohibited on company systems:
- Installing unauthorized or unlicensed software.
- Disabling or circumventing security controls, including endpoint
  protection, screen locks, or network monitoring tools.
- Connecting personal storage devices (USB drives, external hard drives) to
  systems that process sensitive data, unless explicitly approved.
- Transmitting confidential company data to personal email accounts or
  unsanctioned cloud storage services.
- Using company systems to access or distribute illegal content.

## Section 4: Data Handling
Employees must classify and handle data according to its sensitivity
level (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted). Confidential and
Restricted data must be encrypted both at rest and in transit, and must
not be stored on local devices without explicit written approval.

## Section 5: Monitoring
The company reserves the right to monitor use of its systems, including
network traffic, email, and file access, for security and compliance
purposes, consistent with applicable law.

## Section 6: Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and
including termination of employment, and may be reported to law
enforcement where illegal activity is involved.
